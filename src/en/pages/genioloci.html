FAQ

Before you order.

Honest answers. No promises a food supplement can't keep.

How is it different from Jatroděj and Očistelix?

GenioLoci spreads its support evenly across the whole day. Jatroděj focuses on the morning, Očistelix on the evening. Not sure which suits you? Try our three questions.

How long should I take the plan?

Each plan is designed for 30 days. You can carry on for another month if you like. Just stick to the recommended daily dose on the pack. With a subscription, your next pack arrives automatically.

Can I take the plan if I'm on medication?

Please talk to your doctor or pharmacist first, and bring the plan's ingredient list with you. Some herbs can interact with medicines, so we won't guess on your behalf.

Is the plan suitable during pregnancy or for children?

No. Our plans are not intended for children or for pregnant or breastfeeding women.

When will I notice a difference?

We can't promise that, and no honest brand should. A food supplement is not a medicine, and everyone responds differently. The plan is one part of the picture, alongside diet, sleep and exercise.

Can I combine two plans?

We don't recommend it. The plans partly overlap, for example in magnesium, so you could exceed the recommended daily intake. Choose one, or go for GenioLoci, which covers the whole day.

What if I miss a phase?

No problem. Carry on with the next phase at its usual time, and don't take a double dose to make up for the one you missed.
