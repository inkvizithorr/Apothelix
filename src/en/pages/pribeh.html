I survived my own lifestyle. Then I had to fix it.

FOUNDER OF APOTHELIXAGE 37 · WHERE IT STARTED

“At 37, you're supposed to be in your prime. I felt like my battery was running out.”

I wasn't exactly living by the book. Late nights, fast food on the run, the odd drink with friends, caffeine as fuel and constant stress on top. I woke up more tired than when I went to bed. My skin looked dull, my head felt foggy, my mood went up and down and my motivation was at zero.

Like most people, I started buying supplements. I swallowed handfuls of vitamins. I felt no difference. I spent a small fortune without realising I was making a basic mistake.

I didn't understand the biochemistry. I bought minerals in forms the body uses less well. I bought herbs without standardised extracts, so I never really knew how much of the active compound they held. I had no idea that magnesium malate and magnesium bisglycinate are two different things, or that it matters when you take them.

It took me years to get it. I started studying glutathione, liver enzymes, steroidal saponins and the circadian rhythm. I built a daily routine from the best ingredients I could find, from 80% silymarin to pure chelates. And I changed how I eat, sleep and live, too.

It didn't happen overnight. Biology doesn't work like that. But little by little, I started to feel different. I woke up with a clearer head and wanted to get things done again.

I created Apothelix so you don't have to repeat my mistakes or spend money on illusions. I put together complete plans, built on exactly what I spent years fine-tuning for myself.
